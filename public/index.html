A little closer, in three steps.

  1. Choose what to share

    Use Share my screen to select a tab, window, or display. Sharing starts only after you make a choice.

  2. Send the invitation

    Copy your link and send it to your viewer. Open viewer lets you test in another tab.

  3. Approve and collaborate

    Accept the viewer’s request. End session stops the stream for both of you.

Just exploring?
